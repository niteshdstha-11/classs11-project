Personality

Ryomen Sukuna is arrogant, sadistic, and highly confident in his abilities. He views most humans and sorcerers as insignificant and enjoys demonstrating his superiority. Despite his cruelty, he possesses a sharp intellect and deep understanding of jujutsu principles.

He is highly intelligent and analytical in battle. Unlike mindless curses, Sukuna thinks strategically, quickly analyzing opponents’ techniques and weaknesses. He rarely wastes energy and prefers efficient, lethal attacks that end fights instantly.

Sukuna also has a cruel sense of enjoyment in combat. He takes pleasure in breaking opponents mentally and physically, often mocking them during battle. This sadistic enjoyment makes him even more dangerous, as he is unpredictable and fearless.

Despite his arrogance, Sukuna respects strength. If an opponent proves interesting or powerful, he may acknowledge them briefly — but never sees them as equals.

His presence alone creates fear and pressure, even among experienced sorcerers. Sukuna embodies pure chaos and domination, making him one of the most feared beings in the Jujutsu world.

Unlike mindless curses, Sukuna acts with purpose and strategy. He rarely wastes energy and chooses his actions carefully, making him one of the most dangerous antagonists in the series.
